### A.2 11 Jackpot Carryover

When set to "Yes", a player's uncollected Jackpot will carry over to subsequent multiball. If set to "No" the Jackpot value will reset to 10 Million at the start of each multiball. Anytime the Jackpot is collected, it automatically resets to 10 Million.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |

### A.2 12 Million Plus Memory

This determines whether the side ramp Million Plus feature carries over from ball to ball or is reset to one million at the start of each ball.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |

### A.2 13 Million Plus Maximum

This sets the maximum value for the side ramp Million Plus feature. Subsequent shots will award this maximum value without increasing.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Range | 5,000,000 - 20,000,000 |

### A.2 14 Hurry Up Start

This is the starting (maximum) value for the Thing "Hurry Up" quick multiball feature. The award will start at this value and count down to 3,000,000.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Range | 10,000,000 - 20,000,000 |

This allows the Mansion awards to be awarded by balls entering the Swamp kickout.
