## A8 Shopping out the playfield

Owner's notes, after Lou Perazzoli's 2002 shop-out walkthrough on PinWiki, reworded and tied to this app's part names. A shop-out is the full strip: every plastic, ramp, post and guide off, so the playfield, the inserts and the corners can be cleaned and waxed properly. For an office machine that is once every few years, or the day the LED kit goes in, since the plastics come off for that anyway. The monthly and half-yearly rounds on the Care page cover everything short of this.

### Before the first screw

- A paper cup per assembly, named with a marker: slingshot plastics, swamp plastics, and so on. Screws and nuts go in the cup, or better, back into the part they came out of.
- A photo of every step before it comes apart. Label every connector you unplug with a letter.
- A white towel on the cabinet floor catches dropped screws.
- The three balls out first.
- Cleaning products are the Care page's: Novus 1 on the playfield and plastics, carnauba wax, nothing with ammonia or petroleum on the clear coat or the plastics. The original guide reaches for glass cleaner on the inserts; a dry brush first, then a barely damp cloth, does the same without the risk.

### Order of removal

1. Playfield up. Unplug every wire that runs through a hole to the top side (ramp flashers, ramp optos, the Thing box) and check each one can come up through its hole freely.
2. Slingshot plastics. There is a washer on top of the ramp bracket.
3. The wire ramp. Its wires only come up after the swamp plastics are off.
4. The swamp plastics and the ones below them come off as one linked piece; no need to separate them. Double rubber rings under these plastics.

![Lower playfield with the slingshot and swamp plastics off. Photo Lou Perazzoli, via PinWiki; rights remain with them.](fig/app108-slings-removed.jpg)

5. The plastics by the lower flippers, then the outlane star posts: double rings on a 1-3/4-inch bolt. Under the red plastic with the braid are three star posts with double rings: the top one a 2-inch post with 5/16 inch showing above the nut, the left one a 1-3/4-inch pan-head screw, the bottom right a 2-3/8-inch post with 5/8 inch of screw showing for the ramp.
6. The electric chair: bulbs out, then lift it straight up.
7. The rose, amber and clear jet bumper caps. The red and blue ones wait until the bear kick ramp is off.
8. The STOP-sign plastic by Thing, then the star posts under it: the lower a 2-inch post with 5/16 inch showing, the upper a 1-3/4-inch pan-head screw.
9. The plastic over the Thing Flips flipper.
10. The bear kick ramp. It can be done with the playfield in its hanger, but it is much easier with the playfield lifted out: stand it at about 60 degrees, lift straight up (it is heavy, take two people), set the hanger bracket on top of its post, put a towel on the front rails and lower the playfield pulled about 4 inches out of the cabinet. Left in the hanger, you need a stubby Phillips for the ramp screws, the ball guides are awkward, and the Thing box in the rear corner is out of reach.
11. The red and blue jet caps, then the plastics under the bear kick ramp. The long green plastic on the left has three washers on the posts under it. The acid-bottle plastic has a 2-inch bolt on its right side with a washer on top of the plastic. Both star posts by the vault are 2-inch posts with 5/16 inch showing.

![The playfield with the bear kick ramp off. Photo Lou Perazzoli, via PinWiki; rights remain with them.](fig/app108-ramp-removed.jpg)

12. The bookcase sign: two screws on top.
13. The ball guides. Some have nuts underneath. Easiest: take everything off the far back of the playfield, then put the playfield back in its hanger so it can be raised and lowered without worry.
14. The Thing box: playfield up, the metal cover off (two screws at the top, slide it back; the edges are sharp), then lift the plastic box straight up. The hand comes off its mechanism with two screws.

![Every plastic off, the Thing box out. Photo Lou Perazzoli, via PinWiki; rights remain with them.](fig/app108-plastics-removed.jpg)

15. The rear ball guides, so the corners can be cleaned.

![Stripped and ready to clean, lower half. Photo Lou Perazzoli, via PinWiki; rights remain with them.](fig/app108-stripped-lower.jpg)

![Stripped and ready to clean, upper half. Photo Lou Perazzoli, via PinWiki; rights remain with them.](fig/app108-stripped-upper.jpg)

16. Under the playfield: the ball trough out, and its switch off the plastic trough so the trough can be cleaned properly. The lamp boards and plastics that hold bulbs off, to reach the inserts from below. Inserts: dry brush first for the loose dirt, then damp.

### Going back together

- Reverse order. The plastic with the telephone on it is the hard one: put the rear screw in, slide the lower plastic under it, and reach the front screw between the plastics with a thin magnetic Phillips; the ball guides block a normal one.
- Once every bulb is in and before the plastics go on, run T.10 Lamp Test. Finding a dead lamp in the back corner after everything is back is the worst outcome of a shop-out.
- Before the glass: T.1 Switch Edges with a ball, then the solenoid test, then a game.
