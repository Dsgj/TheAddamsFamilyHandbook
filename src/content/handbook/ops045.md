### A.2 16 Free Thing Lamps

This gives each player this many lamps in the T-H-I-N-G spell out at the start of each game.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Range | 0 - 5 |

### A.2 17 Raise Dead Hits

This is the number of hits on each bumper required to complete the bumper for the "Raise the Dead" mode.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Range | 3 - 9 |

### A.2 18 Train Wreck Hits

This is the number of hits required for the 1st Train Wreck. Each subsequent Train Wreck requires 1 additional hit up to a maximum of 6.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Range | 2-4 |

### A.2 19 Special Memory

This determines the Special (which is lit by the "Tour The Mansion" feature) stays in memory from ball to ball

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |

### A.2 20 Disable THING

Set to "Yes" to stop the operation of the Thing hand.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |

### A.2 21 Disable BOOKCASE

Set to "Yes" to stop the operation of the Bookcase.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |

### A.2 22 Train Wreck Memory

This determines whether the Train Wreck progress is maintained ball to ball (yes), or reset every ball (no).

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| yes | Train Wreck progress is maintained ball to ball |
| no | Train Wreck progress is reset every ball |

### A.2 23 Quick Multiball Memory

This determines whether the quick multiball lamp (earned as a Mansion award) carries over from ball to ball

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |
