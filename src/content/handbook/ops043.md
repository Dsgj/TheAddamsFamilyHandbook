## A.2 Feature Adjustments

### A.2 01 Extra Ball Percent

This is used to enter the desired extra ball percentage. The House and Train Wreck extra balls always remain fixed, and the Bear Kick extra ball threshold will automatically be modified to reach the desired percentage.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Range | 15% - 40% |
| Fixed | Set to "Fixed" to disable the automatic percentaging of the Bear Kick Extra Ball. |

### A.2 02 Bear Kick Extra Ball 1

This is the number of Bear Kicks that will light the 1st extra ball. The machine will start with this value and modify it as necessary to achieve the percentage specified in A.2 01. To use a fixed level for the 1st extra ball, set A.2 01 to "fixed" and set this level here.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Range | OFF, 4-12 |

### A.2 03 Bear Kick Extra Ball 2

This determines whether a 2nd extra ball is available at 50 Bear Kicks.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Range | OFF, 30-99 |

### A.2 04 Train Wreck Extra Ball

This is the number of train wrecks that will light the Train Wreck extra ball.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | On/Off |

### A.2 05 Bear Extra Ball Memory

This determines whether extra balls lit from the Bear Kick feature remain in memory from ball to ball.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |

### A.2 06 House Extra Ball Memory

This determines whether extra balls lit from the Mansion feature remain in memory from ball to ball.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |

### A.2 07 Train Extra Ball Memory

This determines whether extra balls lit from the Train Wreck feature remain in memory from ball to ball.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Setting | Yes/No |
